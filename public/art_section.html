How I started and when?

I started 2D art around August 2024, mainly started it because I was interested in a lot of furry artists posting online and such, also coming from a few years of 3D art practices and work.

About 3D art, I started around early 2019, started it completely out of the blue, as I didin't really had any intentions to work with it, simply started it by my own interest for making game assets.
After around a year of practicing and doing my own assets and renders, I got contacted by someone on the ROBLOX platform, as he was interested in my models for his game, so I ended up doing contracted work.
After this, I started working more and more in various different projects with 3D, and developing more skills along the way.

if you want to see all the projects where I've worked, you can check in the [Commissions history & work] tab.
