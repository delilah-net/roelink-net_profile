little about me :P

You can refer to me as Laila, generally active on Discord, not as active/frequently in the day due working constantly on new projects & other stuff.

Both 3D/2D art and web design are my current hobbies, besides ocassional photography (still need to learn more) whenever I go out places.
If you want to see any of my artworks, you can check by going into the Art Section above, or by [clicking here].

I love retro/Y2K/etc types of aesthetics, I think they were the main reason I started learning webdev practices, aside from trying to make it my actual job.
If anything im surprised I managed to learn this much, and I hope to keep learning more as I go.

tech interests

Simply a random list of random tech stuff I have my eye on for the future, although some might need a modern tweak.

[If you look to suport my projects, you can do so in the Support section .]



Mechanical Keyboard Clicker APC F-21

Love old-styled keyboards a lot, although some still have the old connects that are not for most modern cases, which would require a workaround to swap it for a modern one, or maybe getting a proper adapter to USB-A.

Probably better alternatives out there, or already modernized old keyboars for a bit more price.

Avrg. Price: 70-80 EUR~

Old gel mouses / Vintage

Various models of old gel mouses, most of them tied directly to organizations or groups [The one in the image being a life assurance company from the United States.], as well as regular vintage ball mouses from Microsoft.

Many exists out there with either their original connectors which won't work on modern computers unless its supported or adapted, and other more modern variants that are for newer hardware, like Skeuoss.net ones.

Avrg. Price: 50-60 EUR~ [Sometimes less]

Panasonic Let's Note CF-SX2

With a cool built-in ODD [Optical disk drive], that allows you to have several uses for discs and such, I think its one of my favorite designs for a laptop, having tons of resellers online with modified/swapped parts and others.

Really looking forward to get one, as I think I could resume my webdev/coding practices and also drawing outside, as long as I bring my tablet, since I usually just always work/study from home.

Avrg. Price: 130-150 EUR~

SilverStone FLP03

Retro sleeper case from Silverstone, really love these white/beige-ish colors for mouses or keyboards or even cases. Featuring a key that needs to be used in order to power the computer properly, as well as other modifications and displays built in the case.

Surprisingly not as expensive as I though, as there's many seller options around EU, most of them varying from 90 to above in euros.

Avrg. Price: 90-100 EUR~

Vintage Sony DR11 headset

Absolutely love this design that Sony came with, absolutely feels like a different era jump, as these headsets were released all the way back in 1973.

I wasn't able to find listings on this specific item, besides 2 being near around 500ish euros, the headset itself seems pretty limited & hard to find, let alone having a increased price due the low amount of them.

Avrg. Price: 500 EUR~ [Limited]
